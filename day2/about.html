About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple application for capturing and organising notes quickly. It helps you keep track of ideas and reminders before they disappear.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the New note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your saved notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quickly capture new notes.
  • View your saved notes in one place.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Add a new note
Esc Clear the note input
